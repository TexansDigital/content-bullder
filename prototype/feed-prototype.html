    Why a rail, not a grid

    A horizontal rail of 4:5 tiles costs one row of page height, reads as editorial rather than as an ad unit, and sets up the full-screen 9:16 player as the reward for tapping. A grid of vertical tiles eats the page and competes with the article it sits next to.

    FORGE constraints

    • Embed asSelf-contained iframe. Same bundle serves the FanReach webview — one codebase, two surfaces.
    • HeightRail posts its height to the parent so FORGE can size the frame without a scrollbar.
    • OpenPlayer takes over the frame on web; on app it goes full-bleed through the bridge.
